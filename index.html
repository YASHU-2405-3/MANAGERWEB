Clear expectations matter

A good partnership begins with trust, not big promises.

Useful to know

Questions before you apply?

Here is what to expect from the current Creator X Brand process.

Contact the team →
Who can apply?

Creators with a public creator or channel link and brands with a company or campaign brief can submit an application. The team reviews each application before the next step.

What happens after I submit?

Your application is saved for review. If it is approved, the account can use the platform chat for the related conversation. Keep your account email accessible for follow-up.

Does applying guarantee a sponsorship or creator match?

No. An application is an introduction request, not a promise of acceptance, a paid collaboration or earnings. A suitable partnership depends on fit and agreement between both sides.

Does Creator X Brand process payments?

Not currently. Before any work begins, the creator and brand should agree directly on payment method, amount, timing, deliverables and usage rights. Do not start work on unclear terms.

Are social views and campaign analytics connected?

Live account analytics are not connected yet. Any examples shown as illustrations are not real campaign data and should not be treated as verified results.
